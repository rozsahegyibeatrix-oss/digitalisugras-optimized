No paid link schemes, no fake reviews, no link swaps at scale: they get sites penalised.
I did not visit these sites; check each URL and its current listing rules before applying.

## b) Google Business Profile and citation consistency

Use one exact NAP (Name, Address, Phone) everywhere. The values below are **not known to me**; fill from
the owner (`SEO_TODO.md` item 3) and never vary spelling, abbreviations or accents.

- [ ] Business name written exactly: `Digitális Ugrás` (with accents)
- [ ] Address (or "service area: Budapest" if no public address; hide address in GBP if home-based)
- [ ] Phone in one format, e.g. `+36 ...`
- [ ] Website exactly `https://digitalisugras.company/`
- [ ] Email `digitalisugras@gmail.com` (or a domain address, then change it everywhere)
- [ ] Primary category (e.g. marketing agency), services listed with the real prices
- [ ] Hours identical on GBP, Facebook, directories
- [ ] Same short description (HU) and same logo file on every profile
- [ ] Reviews: ask real clients, never buy or write them; reply to all
- [ ] Re-check listings every 6 months for drift

## c) Outreach emails (Hungarian) — TEMPLATES ONLY, do not send without owner approval

Rules applied: addressed to generic public company addresses (`info@`) of businesses, not named individuals;
sender identified; source of address stated; one call to action; opt-out sentence; no commercial claims
that are not on the site. Under GDPR the basis is legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f)); under the Grt.
(2008. évi XLVIII. tv.) direct marketing to natural persons needs prior consent, so **do not use for
sole traders' personal addresses**. This is not legal advice; have a lawyer confirm before a campaign.

## d) Two linkable assets the owner can actually produce

1. **Free "Weboldal-ellenőrző" for local businesses.** A one-page tool where an owner enters their site
   URL and gets a plain checklist: HTTPS, mobile viewport, title/description present, one h1, Google
   Business Profile linked, booking link visible. You already have the checks in this audit and in
   the lead-finder scripts; a serverless function fetching the page and testing ~8 things is a weekend of work.
   Publish at `/eszkozok/weboldal-ellenorzo` (add to `ROUTES`), pitch to targets 25, 34-36.
2. **Original benchmark: "Budapesti kisvállalkozások digitális jelenléte 2026".** Aggregate the scored data
   from your lead-finder pipeline (share of local businesses with no website, no booking link, no
   Google profile), by category. **Publish only aggregate percentages, no business names or contact
   details** (GDPR; the pipeline rule that lead data stays in the EU and is not published). Real numbers
   from a real sample earn citations from targets 23, 26, 27.
